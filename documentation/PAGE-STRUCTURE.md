# Page Structure

- `pages/index.html` — direct retail homepage with nine storefront sections
- `pages/home2.html` — editorial aquascape studio with scene hotspots
- `pages/fish-catalog.html` / `fish-details.html` — filterable species catalog and dynamic care page
- `pages/aquatic-plants.html` — placement-filtered plant store
- `pages/equipment.html` / `product-details.html` — equipment categories and shared product view
- `pages/care-guides.html` / `pages/guides/*` — searchable guide index and six full articles
- `pages/tank-planner.html` — dynamic, size-aware equipment checklist
- `pages/cart.html` / `checkout.html` — persistent bag and honest checkout handoff
- `pages/contact.html` — location status and special-order enquiry
- `pages/about.html`, `faq.html`, policy pages, coming-soon and 404 — supporting content

Global navigation, footer, overlays and page content are composed by `assets/js/main.js`. Catalog records are centralized in `assets/js/catalog-data.js`.
