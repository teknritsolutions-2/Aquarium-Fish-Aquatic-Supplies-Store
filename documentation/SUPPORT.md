# Support and Production Checklist

Before launch, obtain and configure:

- Final legal business name and approved logo
- Verified physical address, map coordinates, store hours, phone and email
- Final currency, prices, tax and inventory source
- Exact-species licensed photography and local optimized assets
- Verified product manufacturer specifications
- Form endpoint and privacy-compliant retention policy
- Shipping, pickup, returns, cancellations and live-animal welfare policy
- Payment/commerce provider if checkout will become transactional
- Final public domain for canonical and Open Graph URLs

For content updates, edit `assets/js/catalog-data.js`. For layout and interaction updates, use `assets/css/style.css` and `assets/js/main.js`.
