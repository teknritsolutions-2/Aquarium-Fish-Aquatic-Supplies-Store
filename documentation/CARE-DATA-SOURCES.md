# Care Data Sources and Method

Species records are conservative editorial starting points assembled around commonly published aquarium ranges. They are not prescriptions or guarantees. Final commercial content should be reviewed against current species accounts and veterinary or professional husbandry guidance.

Recommended reference set for verification:

- [FishBase](https://www.fishbase.se/) — taxonomy, natural distribution, size and ecology
- [Seriously Fish](https://www.seriouslyfish.com/) — freshwater aquarium husbandry profiles
- [Animal Diversity Web](https://animaldiversity.org/) — life history and taxonomy where available
- [NOAA Fisheries](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/) — marine species and conservation context where applicable
- Manufacturer manuals — exact equipment fit, flow and operating limits

Editorial rules:

- Minimum tank figures are suggestions, not universal legal or welfare guarantees.
- Planted-tank-friendly never means all marked species are mutually compatible.
- Freshwater and marine animals are never combined.
- Salinity is shown only for marine species.
- Lifespan language is qualified rather than promised.
- Unknowns should be stated as unknown, not filled with false precision.
